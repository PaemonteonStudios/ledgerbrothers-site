Questions

Before you buy

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. The free live demo on this page is the real General ledger, limited to 15 items. Items stay only while the tab is open.

Is there a cloud version?

Yes. Ledger Brothers Cloud is $9.99 per month and is sold separately. It lets you use the same ledger on several devices after you sign in. The downloadable ledgers are a different product: they work offline and keep your data on your device.

Which browsers does it work on?

It's built and tested on Chrome's browser engine. Testing on Safari, Firefox and Edge, and on Mac and Windows, isn't finished yet. Try it in the browser you plan to use soon after you buy. If it doesn't work for you, the 7-day refund covers you.

Does it work on an iPad or phone?

The layout is designed for touch, with large tap targets and no zooming on input. It hasn't been tested on real iPads and iPhones yet.

How do I back up?

Click Back up now in any ledger. It saves one file with your items, settings and history. Keep it somewhere safe, like a USB drive or your own cloud folder. To restore, open Settings and choose Restore from a backup file.

CSV export is for spreadsheets only and doesn't save your settings or history.

What if I get a new computer?

On the old computer, click Back up now. Copy the backup file to the new computer, open the same ledger there, and restore it from Settings.

Can I bring in my spreadsheet?

Yes. Save it as CSV and import it, up to 500 rows per file. Only a Name column is required.

Do I need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. When you're online it loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and offline it uses your computer's fonts instead.

Can I upgrade from Standard to Pro later?

Yes. Back up your Standard ledger, then restore that backup into the matching Pro ledger. Your items come across with their counts in the first location.

How do updates work?

New versions go to your Gumroad library. Each ledger's Settings shows your version number and the update steps.

Is this accounting software?

No. It counts and tracks stock. It doesn't do bookkeeping or tax.

What's your refund policy?

If it's not right for you, reply to your Gumroad receipt email within 7 days of purchase for a full refund.

Data Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This policy explains where the data you put into the Ledgers lives, who can access it, and what you need to do to keep it safe.

1. Where your data is stored

Everything you enter (items, counts, settings, locations and history) is saved in your web browser's local storage on the device you are using. It is saved automatically after each change. In the downloadable ledgers there is no server, cloud copy or account (Ledger Brothers Cloud is a separate service with its own policy). We cannot see, access, recover or delete your data.

2. What this means for you

  • Your data belongs to one browser on one device. Opening the file in a different browser, on another device, or in a private or incognito window shows an empty ledger.
  • The Ledgers do not sync between devices or users.
  • Clearing your browser's site data, cookies or history, using cleaning tools, resetting your browser, or losing or replacing your device can permanently erase your data.
  • Some browsers, particularly Safari, may remove stored data from sites or files you haven't opened for a while. Back up often if you use them.
  • In some browsers, moving or renaming the ledger file can make it appear as a new, empty ledger.
  • Only the most recent 300 history entries are kept in the browser. Export the history from the History tab if you need a full record.

3. Backups are your responsibility

Use Back up now regularly. It saves one file containing your items, settings and history. The Ledgers remind you if you haven't backed up for 7 days. Keep backup files somewhere safe, such as a USB drive or your own cloud storage. You can restore a backup into the same type of ledger from Settings. CSV export is for spreadsheets only and does not include settings or history. Because we never hold your data, we cannot restore it for you, and we are not responsible for data that is lost.

4. Sensitive information

The Ledgers are designed for inventory. Do not store payment card numbers, passwords, health information, government ID numbers or other sensitive personal information in them. Anyone with access to your device and browser can open your ledger, so protect your device with a passcode.

5. Network requests

The Ledgers make no network requests except loading fonts from Google Fonts when you are online. Your inventory data is never included in those requests.

6. Your clients' data (Agency license)

If you set up the Ledgers for clients, their data is stored on their devices, and you and your client are responsible for it. If you keep copies of clients' backup files, you are responsible for protecting them and for meeting any privacy obligations you have to your clients.

7. Deleting your data

To delete your data, clear the site data for the ledger in your browser settings and delete any backup files you made. Make a backup first if you might want the data later.

Cloud questions

How much does Cloud cost?

$9.99 per month. Cancel any time. You can ask for a refund within 7 days of your first payment.

Is Cloud the same as the downloadable ledgers?

No. Cloud is a separate, optional product. The downloadable ledgers are one-time purchases that work offline and keep your data on your own device.

Where is my data stored?

With our hosting provider in the United States. Details are in the Cloud privacy policy. The downloadable ledgers never send your data anywhere.

I subscribed. How do I get in?

Go to cloud.ledgerbrothers.com, choose Create an account, and use the same email you subscribed with. Confirm the email we send, then sign in.

How do I cancel?

Cancel from your Gumroad receipt or library. Questions: support@ledgerbrothers.com.
